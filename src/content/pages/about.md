I'm Huong Nguyen.

A DevOps Engineer. A husband. A father to a daughter.

I spend a lot of time thinking about life, career, and the downfall of my country.

I started this blog as a way to put those thoughts into words, organize them, improve my writing, and become a better communicator.

Here, I'll write about whatever crosses my mind — work, life, books, society, and everything in between.

I write in both English and Vietnamese, depending on what feels right for each thought.
