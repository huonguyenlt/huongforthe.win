I'm Huong Nguyen.

A DevOps Engineer. A husband. A father to a daughter.

I spend a lot of time thinking about life, career, and the country I live in. My thoughts often go in different directions, sometimes becoming a tangled mess in my head.

I started this blog as a way to put those thoughts into words, make sense of them, and perhaps understand myself and the world a little better.

Here, I'll write about whatever crosses my mind — work, life, books, society, and everything in between.

I write in both English and Vietnamese, depending on what feels right for each thought. Not every post needs a translation. Some things are simply better expressed in one language than the other.
